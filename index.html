Career roadmap

Two paths, five stages. Each step shows the certifications people usually earn at that point and the free resources that help most. Check off your progress below; it is saved on this device and prints as a checklist.

Certifications shown are common milestones, not requirements for every job. CRSP and CRST are granted by BCRSP, CHSC by HSPC, NCSO by IHSA, Gold Seal by the Canadian Construction Association and PMP by PMI; most exams have fees. Mandatory Ontario training is listed under Required in Ontario.

    Construction Management: Occupational Health and Safety (Moodle 12129). Free, verified readings, data and case studies mapped to each week's topics.

    Required in Ontario

    Training, laws and paperwork that Ontario (or federal) law requires, each with a short note on the legal basis. Use it as a teaching checklist, not legal advice; always confirm the current text on e-Laws.

    Webinars & events

    Recurring and on-demand series from Ontario's health and safety associations and industry groups. Dates change every year, so each item links to the organizer's own calendar rather than a fixed date.

    Templates, forms & checklists

    Free inspection checklists, JSA guides, incident and injury forms, toolbox talk libraries and program samples to adapt for class or site.
